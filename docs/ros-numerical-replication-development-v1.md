# ROS numerical replication development evidence

Status: development only. No live projection, historical release gate, admission or publication
path invokes this candidate. A passing result cannot authorize release or model adoption.

`ros-empirical-numerical-replication-v1` measures stability of the same simulation's original
12,288-path prefix against its full 16,384 paths. Its fixed candidate criterion combines an
empirical CDF distance of at most 1/100 with the existing mean-points and expected-games tolerances.
It retains the complete old five-scalar convergence diagnostic and separately records quantile
point sensitivity. An old-method failure remains an old-method failure.

CDF agreement is not a bound on fantasy-point quantile error or predictive accuracy. For example,
the controlled binary-mixture fixture passes the CDF criterion while its median changes by 100
points. The evidence preserves that warning. The DKW bounds describe the fixed simulator mixture
under their stated four-stream independence assumptions; this helper does not establish those
assumptions or football calibration. The declared family size and error budget must cover the
actual predeclared simultaneous claim, including scoring profiles and any additional training
samples. Reusing a cached vector does not constitute fresh independent confirmation.

## Stored-vector linkage

The worker's `pinned-ros-numerical-evidence-v1` adapter accepts:

- The complete original forecast input, including seed, as-of timestamp and exact scoring rules.
- An independently established manifest checksum from the frozen execution manifest.
- A predeclared family size, error budget and protocol checksum.
- The trusted outcome-cache reader and optional cancellation signal.

It captures caller-owned input before I/O, derives the current model's complete physical-input
key, and requires the full 16,384-path cache entry. The cache verifies its manifest, compressed
payload and decoded payload. A missing entry, corrupt entry or changed manifest fails without
simulation, fetching or writing. Restored metadata must match the forecast's scope, weekly model,
scheduled games, strategy and input checksum. Explicit original-seed, original-as-of and derived
seed-hash comparisons reject coherently rewritten metadata even when it has a valid new manifest.

The production component scorer prices every path using the exact canonical league rules,
retains original order before quantile sorting, and validates every component and game count.
The candidate's prefix and full summaries must agree exactly with the production scorer.
The evidence binds the cache manifest, complete-input key, ordered score/game checksums and exact
scoring identity. Standard linear scoring changes reuse the same physical paths; raw weekly
threshold bonuses cannot be approximated from season totals and remain rejected by this adapter.

Receipt verification recomputes from the independent pins and cache; an opaque checksum alone
does not suffice. It captures the submitted receipt before awaiting source reads so concurrent
caller mutation cannot repair an initially invalid receipt.

This linkage authenticates bytes against trusted pins and binds input/scoring identity. It does
not prove that those bytes were actually generated by the declared simulator, authenticate family
selection/completeness, or establish build provenance. Those remain explicit frozen-runner duties.

## Adoption prerequisites

Before any use as a release gate, freeze and verify the complete source/build/protocol and family
manifest, execute the separate fresh-seed confirmation design, and migrate historical/live policy,
stored evidence and API interpretation together under a distinct method version. Preserve all old
failures and expose material point sensitivity to downstream decisions. Same-seed v12/v13 physical
compatibility controls establish a different claim and cannot substitute for fresh numerical or
football calibration validation.
